Why ArcName

A name is the front door to your onchain life

Arc is built for money, agents and apps that move fast. Names make it feel human.

One identity across every Arc app

Wallets are 42-character receipts. ArcName gives you one short, human handle that people, dApps and AI agents on Arc can resolve — pay you, find you and verify you without a single copy-paste.

A portfolio of names you own

Your personal handle, your studio, your brand — register each one as an onchain asset. Every record lives on the Arc ledger with a clear owner, so you can collect, organize and transfer names like the assets they are.

Yours — provably onchain

Registration writes an immutable record and hands ownership to your wallet. No middleman, no renewal treadmill, no platform lock-in: v1 names are yours until you decide to transfer them.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is ArcName the same as ENS?

ArcName is inspired by ENS (that's where the name comes from) but built for the Arc Network: names are registered in ArcNameRegistry.sol on Arc testnet, the native asset is USDC, and v1 has no yearly renewal — a registered name is yours until you transfer it.

What does registering a name cost?

The fee is whatever the deployed registry's price() returns — the deployer sets it in the constructor (0 for free, or e.g. 1 USDC). On top of that, Arc takes gas in USDC automatically. The console shows the live price before you sign.

Is real money involved?

No. Everything here runs on the Arc testnet, where USDC is faucet-issued and has no value. Treat ArcName as a live experiment — grab test USDC from the faucet if you want to play.

Why is the register button disabled?

Registration requires a deployed ArcNameRegistry whose address is set in js/config.js. Until then the app deliberately shows the "deploy to activate" panel instead of faking results. Follow DEPLOY.md to bring it online.
